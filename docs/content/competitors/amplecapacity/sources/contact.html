Customer care

Talk to a person.

hello@amplecapacity.com — for questions, licence keys, billing or anything that has gone wrong.

  1. Email us

    Write to hello@amplecapacity.com. One person reads that address. We aim to answer within two working days.

  2. Before you buy

    Ask us anything about what the app does or does not do. The scan is free, so you can also install it and look at your own results before deciding.

  3. Licence keys

    Your key arrives in the email receipt for your purchase. If it has not arrived, check the address you used at checkout, then write to us with the date of purchase and we will look it up.

  4. Something went wrong

    Tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps. Nothing AmpleCapacity moves is deleted straight away, so if a file is missing it is almost certainly in the vault and can be put back.

  5. Billing and payment

    Payment is handled by our payment processor. For a charge you do not recognise, or a question about tax on your receipt, write to us and we will take it up with them.
