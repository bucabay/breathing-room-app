Refunds and cancellation

All sales are final.

The scan is free and complete, so you can see what the app will do for you before you pay anything.

  1. All sales are final

    AmpleCapacity does not offer refunds. Please use the free scan before you buy: it shows you exactly what the app would clear on your Mac, in full, without payment.

  2. The payment processor may still refund

    Payments are taken through a payment processor, which is the seller shown on your receipt. Their buyer terms say refunds are provided at their sole discretion and on a case-by-case basis. That discretion is theirs, not ours, and this policy does not remove it.

  3. Your legal rights are not affected

    Where the law of your country gives you a right to cancel an online purchase of digital goods, that right stands regardless of what this page says.

  4. Cancellation

    Both licences are a single payment. There is no subscription, nothing recurring, and so nothing to cancel. You are never charged again.

  5. If the app does not work

    Write to hello@amplecapacity.com before asking for money back. Most problems are a licence key that has not arrived or a permission macOS has not granted, and both are quick to fix.
