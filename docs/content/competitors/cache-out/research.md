## Truthful advantages and trade-offs

- **Cache Out fits:** a reader wanting documented controls for several browsers' records, multiple profiles and data categories, with a free offer and vendor-documented local operation. No hosted AI account is part of that documented workflow.
- **Cache Out trade-offs:** macOS 26.2+ requirement, Full Disk Access, potential sign-outs/session-history loss, and incomplete recovery-setting evidence. Clearing download history is not a way to remove large downloaded files. No independent safety, speed or recovered-space results were collected.

- **Breathing Room trade-offs:** non-notarized preview, Python prerequisite and incomplete release/runtime validation; hosted assistance has authentication/data-sharing boundaries and advertised paid care. Do not position it as a proven replacement for Cache Out's exact data types.
