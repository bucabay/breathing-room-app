# Canta alternatives: evidence-based skip

Task: `draft-alternatives-canta-1` · Decision date: **2026-10-22** · Disposition: **skip; no alternatives page created**.

## Decision

Do not create `/alternatives/canta-1/` from the retained evidence. The alternatives intent is several genuinely useful replacements for Canta's documented job: removing Android user and pre-installed apps, including through Shizuku without root. The retained Canta packet verifies that identity and workflow, but contains no verified Android debloating peer packets. Its AlternativeTo discovery edges are CleanMyMac and Mole; they are not evidence of Android substitutes or of an AlternativeTo relationship between Canta and any proposed option.

Mac uninstaller or cleaner records would not fix this gap. They address software removal or storage work on a different platform and cannot be presented as replacements for removing Android packages. A browser-based Canta workflow also does not turn the Mac that hosts a browser into the target device. The device workflow itself remains Canta's documented Android use case.

This is a relevance/evidence exception, not an alias or dead-product finding. Canta remains the canonical owner `canta-1`; no duplicate page or alias skip is appropriate. The older `draftingAllowed:false` is not treated as a screenshot-rights veto: October22 route B would allow a properly sourced diagram without copying competitor UI. It does not, however, supply the missing set of job-relevant verified peers.

- Canta's official homepage says it supports Android 9.0 (SDK 28) and above and states LGPL-3.0. The official repository README describes removing pre-installed or user apps without root using Shizuku. Sources were checked **2026-10-21**; exact excerpts, URLs, and hashes are in `research.json`.

- The README warns that removing critical apps can cause a bootloop requiring factory reset and notes some stubborn Oplus apps. The homepage's “No risk of bricking” language conflicts with that warning; do not repeat a blanket safety claim. No app, device, USB, or recovery test was performed.
- Pricing, latest version, APK integrity, complete approval controls, privacy/backend behavior, and screenshot reuse rights remain unverified in the retained packet. They are not filled in here.
- The official screenshot is link-only: it was not downloaded or visually reviewed, and no reuse permission is established. No competitor image or UI was copied, hotlinked, or redrawn.
- `screenshots.json` records a Breathing Room Space browser preview with illustrative example data, not a native scan or measured cleanup result. It is not a useful visual for an Android alternatives page, and no asset was copied or created.

## Alternatives intent and scope check

The existing packet establishes Canta's target platform and task, but not a roster of comparable Android package-removal tools. The retained CleanMyMac/Mole discovery edges establish discovery only. Existing Mac-focused product packets establish different platform workflows, not Android debloating alternatives. No alternative names, listing relationships, prices, platform details, or feature comparisons are manufactured to meet a count.

The related Breathing Room comparison handoff independently records the same platform boundary. Breathing Room is a macOS maintenance preview, which we build, and not an Android package-removal replacement. Its preview/install and hosted-service limitations are documented in `capabilities.md`; adding it to an Android alternatives shortlist would be misleading.

No distinct alternatives page can be justified without retained official evidence for multiple Android peers and their relevant workflows. Reopen this decision only when such source-backed peer records are available, then assess whether the page can compare meaningful trade-offs without treating directory edges as proof.
