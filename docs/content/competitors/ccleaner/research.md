## Distinct workflow brief

Mac cleanup: browser traces or recurring storage growth?

Decide between deliberate browser-data removal and investigating storage that keeps returning; do not promise deletion solves growth.
- Identify what must be retained: sessions, autofill and originals are not automatically junk
- Read Mac feature scope, not Windows registry/driver claims
- Choose a documented cleanup task or investigate time-correlated growth
- Review location/method/rebuild costs; distinguish Trash from recovered space
- Choose by documented task, with affiliation and preview/hosted limitations

Hand off a local-only decision checklist: browser-data removal, app removal or recurring-growth investigation; no filesystem access, uploads or automatic winner score.
