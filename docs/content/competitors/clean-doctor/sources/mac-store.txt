AI Cleaner Max is the best cleaner app to help you clean up your device's junk in just one click with smart cleanup. 
You can easily remove junk files, similar photos and videos, duplicate contacts, expired calendar events, and expired reminders on your Mac

AI Cleaner Max is a cleaner kit to clean any useless files you want to clean, free up storage fast and easily!

KEY FEATURES:

- Smart clean: One-click AI smart cleanup
- Junk Files Cleaner: Delete system junk, broken data, and caches.
- Uninstall apps: Find and delete unwanted apps completely.
- All-in-One Photo Cleaner: Search and remove similar/duplicate photos, big-size photos, low quality photos
- All-in-One Video Cleaner: Search and remove duplicate videos, big-size videos and low quality videos
- Contacts Cleaner: Search duplicate contact contents, merge duplicate contacts and delete incomplete contacts
- Remove expired calendar events & reminders
- More widgets: custom photo widget, disk storage widget, cleaner Shortcut
- Extras: Sound tone generator, Disk storage monitor
