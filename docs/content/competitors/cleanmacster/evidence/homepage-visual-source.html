Good questions, straight answers.

Which Macs does CleanMacster run on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Is it safe to remove what it finds?

Every scan explains what it found before you clean. Protected system locations are never touched, items that may hold personal data start unchecked, and you can keep anything you like. If you'd rather watch first, turn on dry run and CleanMacster will only log what it would remove.

Will it delete my files?

Only the ones you select. Apps, duplicates and other clutter are moved to the Trash, so you can put them back if you change your mind. There's also a setting that sends everything to the Trash instead of deleting it.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

macOS protects folders such as Mail and Safari data. Without Full Disk Access, CleanMacster can't measure those files or clean them. You can grant or revoke access at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Why does it sometimes ask for my administrator password?

A few maintenance tasks and system-wide background items can only be changed by an administrator. They're marked in the app, and you're asked before anything runs.

Does CleanMacster collect any data?

No. Scans run on your Mac and there's no account. The app only goes online to check whether your apps have newer versions.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once: $9 for one Mac, $19 for three or $29 for five.

How do I get the app after buying?

The app is a free download, and scanning is free too. Cleaning needs a license: your key appears right after checkout and arrives by email. Paste it into CleanMacster to activate.
