In Progress

  / Features currently in development

We're always looking for ways to make CleanMyMac even more valuable to you.

Every improvement starts with user feedback and thorough research. Not every innovation is worth pursuing, so we're intentional about picking and testing new technologies to build the tools you'll actually use.

Here's what we've been working on:

1. Smarter cleanup for AI agents

AI tools and agents create a surprising amount of leftover data, such as caches, logs, and temporary files that quietly pile up. We're working on making CleanMyMac smarter at detecting this AI-generated junk and removing it safely, clearing only the files that won't affect how your tools and agents run.

2. Browser tools

Work doesn't happen only on the Mac; a lot of it goes on in the browser. We're building extensions for Safari and Google Chrome that let you manage tabs and windows, helping you reduce distractions and bring clarity to where you work.

3. Mail cleanup

Inboxes are no longer just for conversations. Over time, they fill up with promos, newsletters, and offers from one-time purchases. CleanMyMac will address exactly that, helping you declutter your inbox, review your newsletter subscriptions, clear out thousands of emails you never knew you had, and reclaim storage space in your accounts.

4. CleanMyMac CLI

We’re bringing CleanMyMac closer to developer workflows with a command-line interface. It lets you run supported cleanup actions without leaving Terminal, making CleanMyMac a more natural part of your workflow. This is the first step toward a broader CleanMyMac experience designed for everyone who codes or vibe codes.

5. Screen-cleaning mode

A clean Mac deserves a clean screen. We’re working on a screen-cleaning mode that covers your display with a dark overlay, making smudges easier to spot while you wipe the screen clean.

Building new things is only half of it. We're improving Cloud Cleanup to make it easier to see what's taking up space, review your cloud files, and decide what you no longer need.

This is just a glimpse of what's ahead for CleanMyMac. Stay tuned — there’s more to come.

The announced features may be subject to change.
