Things you can clean up
with Cleanup :)

Similar Photos

Cleanup finds the best photo in a similar bunch. If you choose to, you can still review all photos before moving to trash.

Duplicates

Duplicate photos are the ones that exactly the same as each other. Delete all duplicates at once and keep the originals.

Videos

Sort your videos by size or date. Delete the ones that occupy the most space.

Screenshots

Delete the screenshots that you don't need anymore. Cleanup also pairs the screenshots that are similar to each other, so that you can review them faster.

Other

These are the photos that don’t belong to any category. Sort them due to size and date. Delete the ones you don't need with simple swipes.
