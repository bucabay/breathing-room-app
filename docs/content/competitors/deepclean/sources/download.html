Open the disk image

Double-click DeepClean-1.3.11.dmg in your Downloads folder. A window opens with DeepClean, an Applications shortcut, and a short Read Me First note.

Drag DeepClean to Applications

Drop the DeepClean icon onto the Applications folder in that window and wait for the copy to finish. That is the whole install.

3

Allow it to open the first timeOnce only

Open DeepClean from Applications. macOS will say it cannot check the app. That is expected for apps from outside the App Store, and here is how to get past it.

macOS 15 Sequoia and later

  1. Click Done on the message.
  2. Open System Settings and choose Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to DeepClean.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

macOS 13 Ventura and macOS 14 Sonoma

  1. In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) DeepClean.
  2. Choose Open from the menu.
  3. Click Open in the dialog that appears.
Why does macOS warn me?

DeepClean needs Full Disk Access to look inside the folders that make up System Data, and App Store apps are not allowed to ask for that. So DeepClean is distributed directly, and macOS asks you to confirm the first time. You do not need to change your Mac's security settings for other apps, and we recommend you do not.

4

Grant Full Disk Access and scan

DeepClean asks for Full Disk Access when it first opens, so it can scan the caches, logs, and developer folders behind System Data. Click the button in the app, switch DeepClean on in the list, and the scan starts on its own.

Everything happens on your Mac. File names, paths, and contents never leave it.
