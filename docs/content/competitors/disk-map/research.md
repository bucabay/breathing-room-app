### Resolve reassuring marketing with the legal risk statement

Homepage reassurance that items moved to Trash can be put back is not a promise that every operation is reversible. [Terms §5](https://diskmap.app/terms/) explicitly describes emptying Trash and asking macOS to delete local Time Machine snapshots: “Some of these actions can’t be undone.” Sizes are estimates; rebuilds may take time/cost or prove impossible. Require review and current tested backups. Do not reproduce “safe” or “nothing lost” as our tested guarantee. D9–D10.

Likewise, “nothing leaves your Mac” must be qualified by optional Ask AI and network update checks. [Privacy §1–3](https://diskmap.app/privacy/) is the more precise source, not an independently audited network result. D11–D12.
