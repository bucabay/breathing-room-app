Mac

Reclaim your Mac's storage safely and easily. Is your Mac running out of space? DiskMaid is the modern, privacy-focused solution to reclaim your storage without the complexity. Whether you are a casual user wanting a quick cleanup or a developer needing to clear gigabytes of build artifacts, DiskMaid gives you the tools to keep your Mac lean and fast. Designed exclusively for macOS with a beautiful native interface, DiskMaid combines powerful scanning algorithms with complete user control. We believe cleaning your Mac shouldn't mean sacrificing your privacy. That's why DiskMaid performs every single scan locally on your device. No data leaves your Mac. Ever. KEY FEATURES SMART SCAN Reclaim gigabytes of space in seconds. Our intelligent Smart Scan safely identifies and removes temporary files that clutter your system without touching your personal documents. • System Junk: Remove user caches and log files that pile up over time. • Developer Files: Safely remove your intermediate build files and package manager caches. • Browser Data: Clear history and cache to free up space and protect your privacy. LARGE & OLD FILES Stop wondering where your disk space went. • Visual Discovery: Quickly spot the largest files taking up space on your drive. • Forgotten Files: Find movies, archives, and installers you haven't opened in years. • Safe Review: Preview files before you delete them to ensure you never lose anything important. DUPLICATE FINDER Don't let duplicate copies waste your storage. • Fast Identification: Our precise hashing algorithm finds exact duplicates, even if they have different filenames. • Smart Selection: Automatically select older versions for deletion while keeping the originals safe. APP UNINSTALLER & REMNANTS Deleting an app isn't always enough. DiskMaid finds the leftovers. • Complete Removal: Locate support files, preferences, and caches that remain after dragging an app to the Trash. • Deep Clean: Keep your library folder organized by removing application leftovers you didn't know existed. PRIVACY FIRST Your data is yours. DiskMaid does not collect any personal information, and all scanning processes happen strictly on your machine. *All features described above requires one time purchase*
