Is it safe?

Yes. DriveScape grades everything safe / review / risky and moves files to the Trash (not a permanent delete), so every action is reversible. You confirm before anything happens.

Only what you choose — caches, real duplicates, developer junk (node_modules, Docker, build caches), big old files, and the Trash. Nothing is touched without your say-so.
