    uid=50_235 heading "WHAT IS DRIVESCAPE?" level="2"
    uid=50_236 "DriveScape is a native disk space analyzer that scans a folder or an entire drive and renders it as an interactive, zoomable sunburst chart, making it easy to see what is taking up space."
    uid=50_237 "
"
    uid=50_238 "Beyond visualizing usage, it classifies files — caches, build artifacts, stale files and duplicates - and how safe each is to remove, then offers categorized cleanup with a staging bin, a review step, move-to-Trash and undo. It includes dedicated tools for developers (node_modules, Xcode DerivedData, Docker, and global caches such as Gradle and Cargo), a duplicate finder, a largest-files list, disk-usage trends over time, and low-disk notifications."
    uid=50_239 "
"
    uid=50_240 "DriveScape runs entirely on your machine - no account, no cloud, no AI, no telemetry. It is available for macOS, Windows and Linux as a one-time purchase (free trial; then $8, up to 5 devices; no subscription)."
