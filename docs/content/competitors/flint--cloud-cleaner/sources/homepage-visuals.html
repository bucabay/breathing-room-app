Flint
Apps · macOS

Take back your Mac's space from the cloud

Flint shows how much space the local copies of your iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox files take up on your Mac — and lets you free it up without deleting anything. Your files stay safe in the cloud.

macOSDownload for Mac
Download on theMac App Store

6 entries

Maps every local copy of your cloud files and shows what's eating your Mac's disk.
