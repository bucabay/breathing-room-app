What LighterMac does, and what it doesn't

LighterMac removes files you select. It sorts what it finds into three groups and will not remove anything in the “never touched” group under any circumstances.

When you press “Free … now”, caches that rebuild themselves on their own are deleted permanently, and the confirmation says so before anything happens; it also offers to move them to the Trash instead. Everything else LighterMac removes is moved to the Trash unless you explicitly choose to delete it permanently. “Empty Trash” deletes everything in your Trash. Deleting permanently cannot be undone. Automatic rules, if you write any, only ever apply to items that rebuild themselves without any action from you.
