## Critical conflict: backup removal is not established

The retained [homepage](https://plumby.app/) says:

> “An old iPhone backup can’t be put back, so Plumby shows it on its own, with its date, before anything happens.”

The newly checked [pricing.md](https://plumby.app/pricing.md) says:

> “Big old files, iPhone backups and installers go to the macOS Trash, where Finder's Put Back returns them, and no setting can change that.”

These are incompatible on a central workflow fact. EULA section 5 says personal files go to Trash but does not resolve this backup-specific contradiction. **Do not recommend backup deletion, promise restoration, or choose the more convenient source.** Obtain consistent version-specific official evidence before drafting the proposed consequence-aware cleanup article. No permission to contact the vendor or test irreversible removal is implied.

There is also an important approval qualification, not a missing-feature attack: [EULA section 5](https://plumby.app/eula) documents optional daily/weekly/monthly **Quick Plunge**, off until enabled, which can clear without asking each time when it would free at least half a gigabyte. Do not repeat the homepage's “clears only what you select” as an unconditional fresh-approval promise. (P07/P08)
