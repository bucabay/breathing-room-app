Frequently asked questions.

How is it different from other Mac cleaners?

Other cleaners estimate a total and can sweep in one click. In Plumby, every item says what it is and what happens if it goes, your own files start unselected, and the space it reports is added up from what actually left.

Don’t the caches just come back?

Some do, and Plumby says which before you clean. Every item carries one of four answers: it comes back over time, it comes back only if that happens again, the space stays free, or your Mac handles it. Old device backups, macOS installers and the leftovers from apps you deleted are in the third group.

Will it delete something I need?

Only if you select it. Anything that might be your only copy starts unselected and goes to the Trash, where Put Back in Finder restores it. An old iPhone backup can’t be put back, so Plumby shows it on its own, with its date, before anything happens.

Why isn’t it on the App Store?

App Store sandboxing blocks the system access Plumby needs to see what’s running, what’s installed, and the caches and leftovers your apps put outside their own folders. It ships directly instead, notarized by Apple before it reaches you.

Does it need my password all the time?

No. You approve its helper once, during setup, and after that maintenance runs without a password. Every action still shows you exactly what it will change.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

So it can look everywhere your Mac keeps files, Mail and Messages included. It works without it, and tells you which places it could not check. It’s one switch in System Settings, under Privacy & Security, and you can turn it off again at any time.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. Everything Plumby finds and explains is free, and so are the first 5 GB it clears and 3 full app removals. You don’t need an account, and nothing expires. If you buy and change your mind, you can get a refund within 14 days.

What happens if I stop paying?

The app keeps working. If you cancel Annual, you keep the version you have and only updates pause. Lifetime has nothing to cancel: you pay once and get every future version.

What happens when I use up the free allowance?

Plumby keeps finding items, explaining each one and showing how much space it would free. What you’ve cleared stays cleared. To clear more, buy a license; your list will still be there.
