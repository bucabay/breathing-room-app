From checkout to activation in three steps

Buy on this page

Checkout opens right here via Gumroad — no Apple ID required.

Get your license key by email

Your key is sent from licenses@storageradar.app within minutes after checkout. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Activate in the Direct build

Open Settings → Purchases & Updates, click Open Paywall, then use Paste Key → Activate License Key. The key is tied to your purchase, not an Apple ID.

What the Direct build does not do

  • It never asks you to sign in on this website.
  • Your purchase and license key never require an Apple ID.
  • It does not auto-delete anything — every removal is your decision.
  • It makes no performance or speed claims.
