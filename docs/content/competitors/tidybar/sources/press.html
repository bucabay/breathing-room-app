Boilerplate

TidyBar is a macOS menu-bar disk cleaner that helps you reclaim storage without the usual baggage. Scanning is free, so you see how many gigabytes you can recover before paying; cleaning is a one-time $25 (no subscription). It removes caches, large files, duplicates and app leftovers, and includes a Developer mode that targets node_modules, DerivedData and other build artifacts most cleaners ignore. Everything it removes goes to the Trash, so nothing is ever truly gone. No telemetry, no account, no Full Disk Access required.
